SC2002 Group Project · Java CLI game in 3 stages

Restaurant Rush

You are the manager. Staff time is the constraint: every staff member gets one task per turn.

Salad $9 · 1 prep unit

Burger $15 · 1 prep unit

Pasta $18 · 2 prep units

18

turns per game

2

tables + a waiting queue

6

customers, on turns 1, 4, 7, 10, 13, 16

To win after turn 18

≥ 4 paid customers

≥ $45.00 revenue

≥ 60 avg satisfaction (paid)

1

Core game

  • Regular customers: −8 satisfaction per waiting turn
  • Waiter, Chef, Cashier: one task each
  • PREPARING → READY → SERVED → PAID
  • At 0 satisfaction a customer leaves without paying

2

VIP & Critic

  • VIP: −5 per waiting turn, pays 10% less
  • Critic: −12 per waiting turn, always orders the slowest dish (Pasta)
  • Same order and service flow for every customer type

3

Host, Combos, Happy Hour

  • Host seats one extra customer a turn
  • Combo: two dishes for 90% of the price, picked at ≥ 90 satisfaction
  • Happy Hour (once): 20% off, +15 satisfaction, 2 turns
